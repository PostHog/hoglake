hoglake: patch (Server)
---

A bulkhead between the Hoglite backends and the PostgreSQL catalogs on the shared request dispatcher (#679). Each backend entry in `HOGLAKE_BACKENDS_FILE` takes a `max_concurrent_calls` (default: half of `HOGLAKE_REQUEST_THREADS` while the process serves PostgreSQL, all of them when only Hoglite is served; a cap that, with its one waiter, reaches the request threads beside PostgreSQL boots with a warning): the most request threads the backend may hold at once. A request holds one permit per operation, taken before its first Hoglite call and kept to its last (a list's parallel reads, a manual maintenance sweep's whole run and the namespace gate's scoped read included), so an operation is refused before it starts or not at all; one request past the cap waits up to 200 ms and any further one is refused at once, with 503 `backend_unavailable` having sent nothing, counted as `hoglake_hoglite_bulkhead_refusals_total`, so a backend holds at most the cap plus one request thread whatever its arrival rate. Each Hoglite call a request makes, other than a write transaction, is capped at what the request has left of `HOGLAKE_COMMIT_LOCK_TIMEOUT_MS` after its queue wait (the queue wait only, not the handler's elapsed time); a write transaction keeps the full `retry_budget_ms`, since an attempt cut short after it was sent would end `commit_outcome_unknown`. A manual maintenance sweep's parallel group workers run under the request's permit and cap. A transport failure against an owner-pinned pod clears the pin and no longer counts toward the pool's circuit breaker, an owner's answer no longer resets or closes it, and neither an owner-pinned attempt nor a call refused for its spent budget before sending spends an open breaker's half-open trial.
