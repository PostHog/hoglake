hoglake: patch (Server)
---

Hoglite reads that were one statement over a whole listing or manifest now stay under hoglited's per-read caps of 100k rows, 64 MiB and 5 s, so they no longer fail with 422 `backend_result_too_large` or `backend_query_timeout` on large catalogs and tables (#686). `listNamespaces`, `listTables` (head and pinned), `listConsumers`, `listOffsets` and the view listing read keyset pages in the order they returned before. A head `listNamespaces` or `listTables` lists the snapshot of its first page: a commit between pages does not show, and a head table listing's later pages take `table_totals` back to that snapshot from the files ended or begun since, read through the `files_ended` index, never from an aggregate over the manifest. When an expiry sweep passes that snapshot before the last page, the head listing starts again from the new head, up to three times. A namespace listing page walks the `namespaces_live` index, and the namespaces dropped after the snapshot are one more scan. Consumer offsets and views are not read at a snapshot, so a listing that spans pages shows an offset or a view changed between pages only in the pages read after it. `listConsumers` pages on the offsets primary key, a walk of its index, and sorts the rows into the listing's order after the last page, so a table renamed or purged between pages neither drops nor repeats its offset; the offset carries the name its page read. A pinned `getTable`'s and a pinned `listTables`'s totals sum the files in ranges of 100,000, a range taking in several small tables at once. `partitionValues` counts them in ranges of 10,000, up to four fields in one statement per range, and merges the counts, with the order and truncation it returned before. It stops counting a field after 100,000 distinct values: that field is truncated, and its values are the most frequent in the files counted until then; once every field has stopped, it reads no further range. The partition stats and partition listing read the live manifest in ranges of 10,000 files, pinned at the head. A range end that does not advance past the range before fails the read instead of looping. `storageUsage` sums file and DV sizes 100,000 rows of each per statement, in id order, so its reads follow the live rows rather than every id ever allocated.
