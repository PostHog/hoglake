---
hoglake: patch (Server)
---

Hoglite runs retirement after each successful manual expiry (`POST /v1/catalogs/{catalog}/maintenance/expire`). Before, nothing reached Hoglite retirement, so a dropped table's live file rows were never retired and `DELETE /v1/catalogs/{catalog}` answered 409 `tables_pending_retirement` forever. Retirement records its own `retirement` run in the maintenance ledger; its failure is logged, recorded as a failed `retirement` run, and does not fail the expiry response. Retirement in this hand-off runs under the expiry purge budget (10 s by default) rather than its own 30 s, so the expiry request stays within client read timeouts; the next expiry continues any backlog. A zero purge budget retires nothing, as it purges nothing. Hoglite retirement now takes `HOGLAKE_RETIREMENT_QUEUE_CEILING` and `HOGLAKE_RETIREMENT_BATCH` (capped at Hoglite's 1,000-row batch) from configuration; before, it always used its built-in 100,000 and 1,000. Because no interval turns that hand-off off, a server whose `HOGLAKE_BACKENDS_FILE` names a Hoglite backend refuses at boot a `HOGLAKE_RETIREMENT_QUEUE_CEILING` below 1 (retirement would never run) or a `HOGLAKE_RETIREMENT_BATCH` below 1 (every Hoglite expiry would fail).
