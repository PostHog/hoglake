# hoglake

The release train: server, trino harness, webui, OpenAPI spec, pyhoglake
and hedgerow, one version per `vX.Y.Z` tag. Written by Sampo from the
changesets in `.sampo/changesets/`; the codec's changelog is
[`pyhoglake-variant-codec/CHANGELOG.md`](pyhoglake-variant-codec/CHANGELOG.md).
Releases up to 1.4.12 are described on their
[GitHub releases](https://github.com/PostHog/hoglake/releases).
